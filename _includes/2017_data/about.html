About Us


Our goal is to challenge ideas of what is possible from today's young people

and support the next generation of leaders, scientists, and entrepreneurs.

  • Our Goal

    We aim to break the Karman Line at 300,000 feet and become the first student team to reach outer space. But our ambitions are much broader than a number.

  • How We're Different

    Operation Space is not backed by any single university, company, or investor. Unlike most student-led rocket projects, we are a group focused on collaboration rather than competition, taking in the brightest and most creative minds from across the nation.

  • Our Timeline

    May, June, and July consisted oconsisted of remote work amongst students- designing the rockets, researching components, and ordering parts. Early August is build week, where we will be assembling and testing the rocket at Vanderbilt University in Nashville, Tennessee. Then, we'll be launching out of Spaceport America in New Mexico in October.
