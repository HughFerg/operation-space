About Us


Our goal is to challenge ideas of what is possible from today's young people

  • Our Goal

    We aim to reach the Kármán Line- widely considered to be the border between our atmosphere and outer space- and break the current student altitude record. But our ambitions are much broader than a number.

  • How We're Different

    Operation Space is not backed by any single university, company, or investor. Unlike most student-led rocket projects, we are a group focused on collaboration rather than competition, taking in the brightest and most creative minds from across the nation.

  • Our Timeline

    May, June, and July will be remote work amongst students- designing the rockets, researching components, and ordering parts. Early August will be build week, where we will be assembling and testing the rocket in the former Northrop Grumman hangar in NY where the original Lunar Module was built.
